## Postdoctoral Position: Statistical Methods for Microbiome and Mental Health

The Sankaran Lab in the [Statistics Department](https://stat.wisc.edu/) of the University of Wisconsin-Madison and the [Wisconsin Institute of Discovery](https://wid.wisc.edu/) in Madison, Wisconsin, has an opening for a highly motivated postdoctoral fellow to support integrative statistical and machine learning analysis of an international, multi-PI study of the microbiome and mental health. The successful candidate will lead the statistical investigation of microbial signatures related to depression and mental health treatment responses in a diverse cohort drawn from the United States and Singapore. These data are generated at a scale and with a multiomic complexity rare in microbiome research: the study will recruit 1,200 participants across the two study sites, treating a subset with a novel mental health intervention, and generating complementary omics, clinical, and psychological measures data. The successful candidate will work closely with Dr. Sankaran and an interdisciplinary research team based out of the Wisconsin Institute of Discovery and [PRECISE Singapore](https://www.npm.sg/) with the goal of integrating metabolomic, epigenetic, and psychological measures with the aim of forming a unified view of the microbiome and depression. A priority of this position is distilling insights from the collaboration into better data analysis methods for integrating multi-omics data. We envision that advances in methods used in this project will have the potential to improve the power and rigor of microbiome integration studies across the field.

Candidates will have an extensive opportunity to develop new technical skills and communicate results to the scientific community through presentations and publications. The University of Wisconsin-Madison offers an intellectually stimulating environment ideal for the researcher interested in the intersection of data science and microbiology. The Wisconsin Institute of Discovery is the interdisciplinary hub of the university, and the Department of Statistics is part of the new College of AI. We are a highly motivated team with varied technical skills and intellectual backgrounds, united by the goal of improving treatments for depression by understanding the biological mechanisms that drive it.

### Job Duties

* Apply and evaluate state-of-the-art multiomics data integration methods for microbiome data.
* Create exploratory views of multi-omics data, including those based on statistical dimensionality reduction by machine learning-based latent space embeddings.
* Rigorously evaluate assumptions and implement simulation-based sanity checks for statistical and machine learning analysis.
* Carry out multi-batch data integration across study cohorts and sequencing runs.
* Applying methods from the interpretable machine learning literature to ensure accurate interpretation of integrative methods.
* Prepare well-documented and highly reproducible statistical analysis workflows.
* Draft, review, and submit manuscripts describing advances in statistical methodology resulting from the collaboration.
* Maintain close communication with the PI and team members describing research challenges and progress.
* Mentor students and team members.

### Qualifications

* PhD in Statistics, Computational Biology, Computer Science or related fields.
* Strong background in statistical or machine learning methods for biological data, particularly in microbiome research.
* Experience developing data analysis software workflows using modern scientific programming languages like R or python.
* Demonstrated skill in communicating complex statistical results precisely and without jargon.
* Strong organizational and interpersonal skills.
